Chapter I

The Beginning

آغاز

31 December 2014. A date that looked like any other date on the calendar. But for us, it became the first page of everything that came after.

We didn't know what the next twelve years would bring. We didn't know about all the places we'd see, the challenges we'd face, the laughs we'd share, or the little moments that would eventually become our favourite memories.

We just knew we had each other.

Chapter II

The Journey

ہمارا سفر

Twelve years isn't just a number. It's thousands of ordinary days that somehow became extraordinary because we lived them together.

There were happy days. There were difficult days. There were days when we probably didn't even know what the next day would look like.

But somehow... we kept moving forward. Together.

Chapter III

The Little Things

وہ چھوٹی چھوٹی خوشیاں

Maybe our best memories aren't the big moments. Maybe they're the small ones. The random conversations. The jokes that only we understand. The late nights. The trips. The food. The arguments that didn't last. The laughter that did.

All those little things quietly became our life together.

Chapter IV

The World We Built

ہماری دنیا

Somewhere along the way, our story became bigger than just you and me.

We built a home. We built memories. We built a family. And then came another little person who made our world completely different.

Our little family. Our biggest adventure. Our daughter.

Our Little World

From two people starting a life together to a family with its own little world...

Looking back, I don't think we could have imagined how much life would change us.

But I wouldn't change the story.

For You ❤️

My love,

Twelve years. Sometimes it feels like yesterday, and sometimes it feels like we've lived a whole lifetime together.

We've changed. Life has changed. But one thing that has remained constant is that somehow, through everything, we've continued to choose each other.

Thank you for being there through the good days, the difficult days, the ordinary days, and all the days in between.

I may not always say everything I feel. But I hope you know how much you mean to me.

If I could go back to 31 December 2014, knowing everything I know today... I'd still choose you.

And I'd choose you again. And again.

Yours, always ❤️
